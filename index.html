The person behind Seni

Created by Avi.

I made Seni with the AIs of science fiction in mind: distinctive presences with character in how they move, speak and respond.

AI can already convey personality in conversation. I wanted to explore how that personality could take shape on screen, through light, movement and expression.

I imagine AI becoming woven into our everyday devices and the physical world. If mine came with a digital body, I’d want it to look something like Seni.

Voice privacy

Seni is an AI voice experience created by Avi Ravisekara. To respond, your microphone audio is sent to ElevenLabs, transcribed, and processed with its service providers, including OpenAI, to generate the conversation.

Call audio saving is off. For new conversations, ElevenLabs transcripts and associated personal data have a zero-day retention period and are scheduled for deletion after the call. Deletion may not happen immediately.

You can mute your microphone or end the conversation using the controls below.

For questions about this demo or your data, contact Avi Ravisekara at avi.sekarr@outlook.com.

Read the ElevenLabs privacy policy and OpenAI API data privacy for more about the providers’ practices.
